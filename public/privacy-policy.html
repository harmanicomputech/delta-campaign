    Who this policy covers

    • Supporters: people who register to receive campaign updates or attend events.
    • Volunteers and staff: people with an account, including coordinators.
    • Polling agents: people assigned by the campaign to observe a polling unit on election day.
    • Visitors: anyone who uses the website or contact form.

    The app is for adults. You must be 18 or older to register or hold an account.

    How we use it

    • To send campaign updates by SMS or WhatsApp, only on the channels you agreed to.
    • To organise volunteers: approving applications, training, assigning walk lists and tasks.
    • To run events: registration, tickets sent by SMS, and check-in at the door.
    • To support polling agents on election day: checklists, incident reports and result-sheet records, reviewed by campaign staff.
    • To answer your messages and data-protection requests.
    • To keep the service secure: sign-in codes, fraud and abuse prevention, rate limiting and audit records.

    We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

    Our legal basis

    We process personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 on these grounds:

    • Consent: for campaign messages. Consent is recorded separately for SMS and WhatsApp, together with the version of this policy you saw, and you can withdraw it at any time.
    • Legitimate interests: for organising volunteers, staff and polling agents who have chosen to take part, and for keeping the service secure.
    • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

    How long we keep it

    • Supporter records: anonymised 36 months after your last contact with the campaign or your last renewal of consent, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
    • Sign-in history: 12 months. Sign-in codes expire after a few minutes and are then deleted.
    • Data exports and evidence packs made by staff: deleted automatically after 24 hours.
    • Election-day records and evidence (result-sheet photos, incident reports): kept while they may be needed for an election petition or other legal process, then deleted. [PLACEHOLDER: retention period agreed with counsel]
    • Audit records: kept as a tamper-evident log for accountability.

    How we protect it

    • All traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
    • Phone numbers and other sensitive fields are encrypted in the database. Duplicates are found using a keyed fingerprint rather than the number itself.
    • Sign-in uses one-time SMS codes. Leadership roles also need an authenticator app, and idle sessions lock themselves.
    • Records you save offline in the app are encrypted on your phone until they sync.
    • Photos used as evidence are stored privately and unchanged, with a fingerprint (SHA-256) that shows they have not been altered. Every view is logged.
    • Important actions are recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.

    Your rights and choices

    You have the right to:

    • see the data we hold about you;
    • correct it;
    • have it deleted;
    • withdraw consent;
    • object to processing;
    • receive a copy of your data;
    • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

    How to use them

    • Stop messages: reply STOP to any campaign SMS. This stops all channels at once.
    • Message preferences and data requests: use the message preferences page. You confirm your number with a code, then choose your channels or request a copy, correction or deletion.
    • On a basic phone: dial the campaign USSD code [PLACEHOLDER: USSD code] and choose Messages & my data.
    • By email: write to the Data Protection Officer at the address above.

    We respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm that you control the phone number before we act.

    Deleting your account

    • Supporters: request deletion on the message preferences page, through the USSD menu, or by emailing the Data Protection Officer. Your record is anonymised. Only a one-way fingerprint of your number is kept, on a do-not-contact list, so that we never message you again.
    • Volunteers, staff and polling agents: ask your coordinator, or email the Data Protection Officer. Your sign-in access ends immediately. Records you created as part of campaign work, such as audit and election-day records, may be kept for the periods above.

    When this policy changes, we update the effective date and version number. If a change affects how we use data you gave consent for, we will ask for your consent again before relying on it.
